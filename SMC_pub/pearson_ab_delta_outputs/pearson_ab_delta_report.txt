=== DELTA-CURVE DEFINITION ===
Within each session, rows are ordered by vr_id, then eeg_id, then trial_id.
Each session is mapped onto normalized progress from 0 to 1 and binned.
Within each (session, bin), values are averaged first.
Then version-level curves are formed by averaging across sessions.
Finally:
  delta_true(bin) = VersionB_true(bin) - VersionA_true(bin)
  delta_pred(bin) = VersionB_pred(bin) - VersionA_pred(bin)
and Pearson(delta_true, delta_pred) is computed across bins.
