=== Why this helps interpret KS ===
KS compares the overall true and predicted distributions after sorting, so it does not preserve which prediction belonged to which trial. That means KS is good for checking distributional mismatch, but not pointwise prediction accuracy.
To see how the KS divergence relates to the predictions themselves, also inspect prediction_error_by_true_quantile.csv. That file shows where the model is over- or under-predicting across the true-value range.
